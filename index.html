WORDHOARD

a greenhouse of words

Fourteen nights of winter remain.
The vault holds the last words.
Tend them: they are the only thing that grows here.

1. Click a glowing garden bed to plant the selected word — or pick another in your pouch first.

2. Water what you want to grow; a lamp speeds a whole row for one night, and fire words feed on it. When frost is forecast, shelter your words — night words don't mind it, and wet words freeze harder. The sky shows tonight and tomorrow: a word still wet when tomorrow's frost comes freezes harder too.

3. After 14 nights, every word that bloomed becomes your poem.

watering also dampens the neighboring beds in the row · grafting two stems grows a word that never existed · whatever blooms stays in the thaw poem
